    Frequently asked questions.

    Not sure where to begin? These answers explain what Rusetat can help with and what to expect.

    Ask us about your plans →
    What can Rusetat Migration International help me with?

    We support tailored travel planning, concierge and ground logistics, corporate and bleisure travel, South African visa and permit applications, international visa assistance, migration guidance, document organization, and relocation landing support.

    Can you guarantee a visa, migration outcome, or booking?

    No. Visa and migration decisions are made by the relevant authorities. Travel services and reservations depend on availability and supplier terms. We help you prepare and plan, but cannot guarantee a decision or booking.

    Is your visa or migration guidance legal advice?

    Our website describes informational and administrative support, not legal representation or legal advice. Requirements vary by destination and can change, so confirm current rules with the relevant official authority or a qualified professional.

    Which South African permits and international visa destinations do you assist with?

    South African categories include Relative, SA Life Partner, Retirement, Medical, Tourist, Treaty, Business, Study, Corporate, and Critical Skills Visas, General Work Permits, and Permanent Residence. International visa assistance is available for Canada, the USA, Poland, Denmark, Australia, Finland, Germany, the United Kingdom, and Dubai (UAE). Requirements and assistance scope depend on the application; official authorities determine eligibility and outcomes.

    Can you help after a visa is approved?

    Yes. Our soft landing support can include neighborhood orientation, housing-search preparation, school-search starting points for families, and a settling-in checklist. The exact support is agreed with you before proceeding.

    Do I upload documents through this website?

    No. The document preview only lists selected filenames in your browser; it does not read or upload file contents. Please don’t send sensitive documents until you have confirmed a suitable, secure process directly with Rusetat.

    How do I get started, and where are you located?

    Complete the enquiry form and choose whether to send it using your own email or WhatsApp app. Our office is at 269 Von Willich Avenue, 66 Corporate Office Park, Centurion, South Africa.
