Privacy & personal information

Privacy Notice

Last updated: 31 October 2026

This notice explains how the Rusetat Migration International website handles information when you browse the site or prepare an enquiry. It is intended to support transparency under South Africa’s Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). It is not a certification of legal compliance.

Responsible party and contact

Responsible party: Caroline Muzambi, for Rusetat Migration International.

Privacy requests and questions: admin@rusetatmigration.com or +27 72 155 5208. Ask for the person responsible for privacy matters.

Information you choose to provide

The personal and corporate enquiry forms can include your name, email address, organization, the service you are interested in, destination, timeframe, group size, travel preferences, and any message you choose to write. Please provide only what is needed for your enquiry.

Fields marked as required must be completed for Rusetat to prepare and respond to your enquiry. Other fields are optional; leaving them blank may limit how specifically Rusetat can respond.

Do not enter passport or identity numbers, financial or medical information, document contents, or another person’s personal information in these forms. The website does not provide a secure document-submission service.

Why and how enquiry information is used

Rusetat uses enquiry details to respond to your request and provide the travel, visa-preparation, migration-guidance, corporate-travel, or relocation support you requested. Rusetat does not use these enquiries for marketing.

The forms run in your browser. This static website has no enquiry-submission backend and does not transmit or store form answers when you fill them in. When you select Email, your device opens an email draft containing the enquiry. Rusetat receives it if you choose to send it. Your email provider may process the draft or message under its own terms.

When you select WhatsApp, the site opens a WhatsApp chat without attaching the prepared enquiry. You decide whether to paste and send any message. If you do, Meta/WhatsApp will process the information under its own privacy terms and may process it outside South Africa.

The chatbot uses preset answers in your browser. Questions and replies are not sent to Rusetat by the chatbot.

Website hosting and technical requests

Your browser connects to the website’s hosting provider to load these pages. As with ordinary web hosting, the provider may process your IP address, requested page, browser details, and request time in technical or security logs. The hosting provider is not identified in the website files; Rusetat should confirm the provider, its retention practices, and any cross-border processing before publication.

Optional external images, maps, and links

Stock photographs are hosted by Unsplash. They remain off until you choose “Allow images.” If allowed, your browser requests those images from Unsplash, which may receive your IP address and browser request details. Your choice is saved only in this browser’s local storage; you can change it at any time using the external media settings on the site.

The office map is not loaded until you select “Load Google Map.” Loading it connects your browser to Google Maps, which may receive your IP address and browser request details and may use its own storage or cookies. Google Maps, WhatsApp, and social-media links are third-party services; when you choose to open them, their providers’ privacy terms apply. External providers may process information in other countries.

The website does not intentionally use analytics or advertising trackers. It does not set a first-party analytics cookie. Local storage is used only to remember your external-image preference.

Document preview

The optional document preview reads filenames and file sizes in your browser to make a local checklist. It does not read document contents, upload files, or attach them to an enquiry. Avoid selecting sensitive files, particularly on a shared device.

Retention and security

Rusetat’s stated retention period for enquiries is three months from receipt, after which enquiry information is deleted. This period should be applied to information received by email or WhatsApp and any business copies. The website itself does not store or retain enquiry submissions.

Use a private device and account when preparing an enquiry. Information sent through email or WhatsApp is handled by those providers and is subject to their security, storage, and retention practices.

Your choices and rights

You may ask Rusetat about access to or correction of your personal information, or request deletion or object to processing where POPIA allows. You may also change or withdraw your choice to load optional external images using the control below. To make a privacy request, email admin@rusetatmigration.com. You may lodge a complaint with South Africa’s Information Regulator.

Changes to this notice

This notice may be updated when the website or Rusetat’s information-handling practices change. Please review the date above when returning to the site.

For urgent questions about how your enquiry is handled, contact Rusetat directly before sharing personal information.

Optional external images We use images hosted by Unsplash. Allow them to load, or continue without them. The image provider may receive your IP address and browser request details. Change this choice anytime in the footer.
